How long does Saudi Umrah visa processing take?

On the website now: Saudi Umrah visas are typically processed within 5–7 working days, and we have a 100% approval rate. The visa fee, biometric appointment and document attestation are all handled by our team, so you do not need to visit the consulate.

On the website now: Every package includes Saudi visa processing, insurance, documentation and 24/7 on-ground support. Hotels, transport, flights and ziyarah depend on the package: each package lists exactly what it includes, and some are offered without an air ticket.

When is the best time for Umrah?

On the website now: Umrah can be performed at any time of year except during the Hajj season. The last 10 nights of Ramadan are the most sought-after time, so ask us early about Ramadan dates. The cooler months, November to February, are gentler for elderly pilgrims.

A8

What is Qafila e Shoq?

On the website now: Qafila e Shoq is a spiritual journey programme led by Pirzada Burhan uddin Ahmed Usmani Sahib (Islamic Scholar). It is built around four pillars: Ibadah (worship), Riyazat (spiritual discipline), Tarbiyat (development) and Siyahat (sacred travel). It is not a regular travel package: seats are limited and by application only.
