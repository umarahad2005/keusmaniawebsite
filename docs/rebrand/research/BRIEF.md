## 4. Reference deconstruction (raw files saved in ../ref/)
### rialto.com.au (ref/rialto.html, rialto-theme.css, rialto-theme.js, rialto-plugins.js)
- WordPress custom theme "rialto-2026". Fonts: Gotham HTF Light (body) + Sharp Grotesk Bold 09 (display) — i.e. light grotesk body, heavy condensed grotesk uppercase display.
- Hero: `<section class="module module--hero">` containing `component component--video` with `video--desktop-1` → https://rialto.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/hero1-1.mp4 (plus vid3.mp4, vid5.mp4 further down). Fullscreen autoplay muted loop video, copy over it: eyebrow "CELEBRATING 40 YEARS / A MELBOURNE ICON", headline "AMBITION LIVES HERE".
- Motion grammar: IntersectionObserver-driven `fade-in fade-bottom` classes with `--fade-duration: 0.75s`, easing `cubic-bezier(.165,.84,.44,1)` (easeOutQuart). Swiper for sliders/carousels (fade + cube effects present). No GSAP/Lenis. So the "feel" the owner likes = video hero + quiet 750ms easeOutQuart fade-ups + generous whitespace + uppercase grotesk hierarchy + image-led modules.
- Homepage order: hero (video) → What's On cards → Perform Well (amenities) → Taste of Success (dining) → Connected Community → Shared Vision → Join an Icon (form) → footer.

### edolus.com (ref/edolus-*.js, edolus-styles.css)
- NOT a DOM site: a PlayCanvas WebGL application (playcanvas-stable.min.js, scene 2509662.json), bg #1d292c. The whole site is a real-time 3D scene.
- Its loading screen (ref/edolus-__loading__.js, well commented) is the transferable gem. Sequence: (1) full black; the logo DRAWS ITSELF on a canvas while a loading % counts 20px below it; (2) at 100% the % vanishes, then the black OPENS IN TWO BANDS (top/bottom curtains) revealing the live scene (background video); (3) the logo GLIDES to its header position (same size/margin as the real header logo), title + CTA fade in; (4) CTA click → overlay manager fades in underneath with its logo on the exact same pixels, then the splash is cut in one frame (no blink). Min black hold 3000ms, fake-fill 3500ms, eased % counter (0.10 per frame), hold at 99% until ready. Replay via sessionStorage flag skips counter.
- Transferable: logo self-draw preloader, two-band curtain reveal, logo-to-nav FLIP handoff, seamless overlay handoff, dark immersive backdrop, slow cinematic camera drift.
